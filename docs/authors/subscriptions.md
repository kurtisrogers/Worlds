# Subscriptions & Chapter Unlocks

## Subscription tiers

Readers subscribe monthly to a tier on a specific story:

- **Bronze** — entry-level supporter access
- **Silver** — mid-tier exclusive chapters
- **Gold** — premium early access

## Chapter unlocks

Authors can set a one-time unlock price on individual chapters. Readers who don't want a subscription can pay per chapter.

## Platform fee

Worlds retains **2%** of each transaction for operating costs. The remainder goes to the author via Stripe.
