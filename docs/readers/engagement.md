# Comments & Reactions

Readers can engage with chapters they've unlocked.

## Reactions

Four reaction types per chapter:

| Reaction | Emoji |
|----------|-------|
| Like | 👍 |
| Love | ❤️ |
| Insightful | 💡 |
| Fire | 🔥 |

One reaction per reader per chapter. Click again to remove, or click a different reaction to change.

## Comments

Signed-in readers can post comments (max 2000 characters) below chapter content. Comments appear in chronological order.

## Implementation

Powered by the `engagement` app with HTMX for instant updates without page reloads.
