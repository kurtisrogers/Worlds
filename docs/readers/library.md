# Your Library

The Worlds library uses a Kindle-inspired layout to organize your reading life.

## Tabs

| Tab | Contents |
|-----|----------|
| **All** | Saved books, subscriptions, and books from followed authors |
| **Saved** | Books you've added to your collection |
| **Subscriptions** | Books you subscribe to (Bronze/Silver/Gold) |
| **Authors** | Books from authors you follow |

## Saving books

On any story page, click **Add to library** to save a book. Click again to remove it.

## Following authors

Click an author's name to visit their profile, then **Follow author** to see their releases in your library.

## Cover art

Authors upload portrait-oriented cover images (2:3 ratio recommended) from the story manage page.
